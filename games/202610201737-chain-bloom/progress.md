# Chain Bloom progress

Plan: a chain-reaction puzzle. Drifting spores float around; the player plants one bloom per level and the blooms spread to pop spores in a chain. Reach the target count of each level.

## Done
- Core mechanic: one click per level, chain reaction of blooms, goal count per level.
- 10 levels with growing spore count and speed, score multiplied by chain length, retry/restart (R) and win screen.
- Tested headless in Chromium: no errors, chains clear level 1.
- Added to root README.

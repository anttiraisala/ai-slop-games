# Chain Bloom

A one-click chain-reaction puzzle: plant a single bloom and watch it spread through drifting spores.

## How to play
Each level has drifting spores and a goal count. Click once to plant a bloom. Spores touching a bloom pop and become blooms too. Pop at least the goal number of spores to advance; otherwise retry. Longer chains score more. Clear all 10 levels to win.

## Controls
- Mouse click: plant the bloom (the dashed circle shows its reach)
- R: restart the current level

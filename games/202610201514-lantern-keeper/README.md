# Lantern Keeper

Survive the night as a dimming lantern until dawn.

**How to play:** Your light shrinks as oil burns. Collect glowing orange oil drops to refill it. Shadows creep toward you; get close with a bright light and they burn away, but if one touches you it's over. Survive 90 seconds to win.

**Controls:** WASD or arrow keys to move. Space/Enter/click to start or restart.
